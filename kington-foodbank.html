Getting help

If things are tight this week, you're welcome here.

To get an emergency food parcel you need a referral voucher. These can be obtained from:

  • The local GP surgery
  • Kington Primary Schools
  • Local Churches
  • Homestart
  • Health Visitors
  • Social Services
  • Citizens Advice
  • Housing Associations
  • Turning Point
  • Places of Welcome (Oxford Arms, Wednesday mornings)

Just come along to the Parish Hall on Church Street with your voucher during opening hours. A volunteer will meet you, and you can take away a bag of food to see you through.

If you have no agency that can refer you, please phone or text the number below.

Information and help can also be obtained at Zero Waste which runs on a Monday 12:15 - 13:00 and Fridays 10:00 - 11:00 at the Parish Hall

Donating food

What the shelves are short of

Tinned and dried goods keep well and go furthest. A few items from the list below make a real difference to a family's week.

  • Tinned meat & fish
  • Tinned vegetables
  • Tinned fruit
  • Tinned soup
  • Pasta & rice
  • Pasta sauce
  • Breakfast cereal
  • UHT / long-life milk
  • Tea & coffee
  • Sugar
  • Biscuits
  • Toiletries & soap

Where to drop off

Bring donations to the Parish Hall, Church Street, on a Friday between 11:30am and 1:30pm, when a volunteer will be there to take them in.

For a different time, or to arrange a collection, contact us first:

info@kingtonfoodbank.org · 07794 439644

Donations can also be left at the Co-op.

Giving time or money

Other ways to help

Volunteer on a Friday

We're always glad of extra hands to sort donations, pack bags and welcome visitors. It's a couple of hours a week, and everyone's shown the ropes on their first session.

Give money

Cash donations let us buy exactly what's short at the time — especially eggs, bread and long-life milk, which runs out fastest. Get in touch and we'll let you know the best way to give.
